# The Classics

The Classics is a guided reading desk for following a ten-year Great Books path and joining the Great Conversation through reading, notes, research, and drafted reflections.

## Language

**Reader**:
A person using The Classics to follow the Great Books path, track reading progress, keep notes, and open supporting resources.
_Avoid_: student, customer

**Great Conversation**:
The long-running public exchange of ideas across classic works, authors, and readers that this app helps the reader enter.
_Avoid_: course, curriculum content

**Ten-Year Great Conversation**:
The multi-year reading path inspired by Mortimer Adler and Robert Hutchins for engaging the Great Books over roughly ten years.
_Avoid_: syllabus, class plan

**Great Books**:
The canon of works represented in the app's library and reading plan.
_Avoid_: content, lessons

**Syntopicon**:
The idea and term index that organizes recurring themes across the Great Books.
_Avoid_: dictionary, generic glossary

**Library**:
The reader's browsable catalog of Great Books and related metadata.
_Avoid_: archive, bookshelf

**Bookclub**:
The app's guided reading-plan area where the reader follows the ten-year path and tracks progress.
_Avoid_: class, course

**Great Author**:
An author represented in the Great Books catalog and used as a navigation point into the reader's research.
_Avoid_: writer profile

**Work**:
A single book, essay, dialogue, play, or other readable item in the Great Books catalog.
_Avoid_: item, content

**Reading Progress**:
The reader's status, dates, and current reading action for a Work.
_Avoid_: completion data, task state

**Reading Stage**:
A named step in the reader's approach to a Work, such as before-reading, during-reading, or after-reading work.
_Avoid_: assignment, lesson step

**Note**:
A reader-owned observation, excerpt response, question, or draft fragment connected to reading.
_Avoid_: annotation when referring to the app's user-created note object

**Conversation Desk**:
The reader's private workspace for shaping notes and reading context into their own contribution to the Great Conversation.
_Avoid_: AI chat, comments

**Glossary**:
The app surface for Syntopicon terms and where those terms appear across Works.
_Avoid_: dictionary

**Resource Link**:
An external search or destination that helps the reader research a Work without the app becoming a teaching site.
_Avoid_: lesson, internal content

**Getting Started Guide**:
The onboarding surface that helps a reader understand how to navigate the app.
_Avoid_: tutorial course

**Study Timer**:
The reader-controlled timer for supporting focused reading sessions.
_Avoid_: productivity system

**Donation**:
An optional way for readers to support the site financially.
_Avoid_: subscription, paywall

**Cloud Sync**:
Optional cross-device persistence for a reader's private app data.
_Avoid_: publishing

**Reader Data**:
The reader's notes, progress, settings, and synced private state.
_Avoid_: customer data

**Public Voice**:
The reader's outward-facing interpretation or argument shaped from their reading and notes.
_Avoid_: generated content
