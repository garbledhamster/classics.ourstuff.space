# Original Idea

## Raw Idea

I already built out most of this fyi, this is for your alignement knowledge we will build later. The original idea is to have a website that is built for helping me follow the 10 year conversation from Mortimer Adlers Syntopicon, that outlines provides a path to reading the great books of the western philosophy.  The goal is to provide a dashboard for myself that I can keep going back to, to help me keep track of what I'm learning.  The fisrst part I envision is a "Library" of sorts that contains all of books I have / are mentioned in the site. Then an area for keeping track of the books I'm reading, I'm thinking "Bookclub" for this one.  Then an area for "Authors" to live from the library to help me learn about the authors quickly. The next page in my head is a way to provide my own voice to the great conversation, "Conversation Desk", this idea is I can use ai in the conversation desk to help me draft my idea into the great conversation, using the ntoes I've taken in the app to help me write what I've learned.  An area for managing all of the notes that, something that makes the notes as important as the learning. A glossary of all the terms and where tey come up, pulled from the syntompicon terms. A getting started guide for users to learn how to use and navigate the site.  A donate option to provide poeple the oppurtuntity to give money if the like the idea of the site. A time for reading or whatever.  A dark/light theme button to make night reading a little easier. 

## Target Users

The intention of this site is to be for myself something I would like to use to follow the great conversaiton, with the added effect of something others can use for themselves, in a way my way of giving back to this interest I have.  Anyone interested in philosophy is welcome here, and the goal is to bring my own ideas about what I'm reading in to the public view.

## Core Outcome

At the end of the day the site is designed to be a guided great conversation and learning tool a space for fleshing out thoughts and what not.

## Non-Goals

The project should never be a "teaching website" it's whole purpose is a point website, to poin me in the right direction, this is done by giving the books dynamic buttons that just open up in various places like google, youtube, goodreads, all the places I usualy go when I'm trying learn about a book, this elminated that and gives every book a single dashboard,  I had thought something better then a list in obsidian that I'm checking off, something more permenant that I know will survive a subscription running out and that I fully control from source to front end.  It should never try to have anything of "teaching" value in it, it's goal is to steer me toward the right "content" online the best "content" for that book.

## Constraints

The site will live in github pages, functions will live in firebase, auth will live in firebase, any montery transactions will be passed through stripe. Cloudflare has the dns, and mxroute has the email.  I put everything to *.ourstuff.space so this is classics.ourstuff.space. It supports firebase Authentication and I can sign in already, storing data goes into firebase documents secured for the users and encrypted with the goal of being gdpr secure. I want users to be in full  control of their data on my sites and protect them as much as possibel secai+ and pentest+.
