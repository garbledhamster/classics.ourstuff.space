# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Readers using The Classics to browse a Great Books library, follow a ten-year reading plan, track progress, keep notes, and open supporting research resources while reading.

## Product Purpose

The app turns a large classics curriculum into a usable reading dashboard: readers can find works, move through the Bookclub plan, mark progress, capture notes, and use guided resource links without losing their place.

## Brand Personality

Scholarly, direct, disciplined. The interface should feel like a working reading desk: dense enough for repeated use, but clear enough that progress and next actions are always visible.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-page composition, decorative academic nostalgia, hidden controls, and action panels that make readers hunt for basic tasks. Avoid visual treatments that make the app feel less like a tool.

## Design Principles

- Keep the reading task visible.
- Prefer durable controls over decorative presentation.
- Make the curriculum navigable at multiple scales: library, year, author, work.
- Preserve the newspaper-like visual identity already in the app.
- Let guidance support the book without replacing direct reading.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible focus states, high contrast text, reduced-motion friendly transitions, and checkbox/select/button affordances that remain usable on mobile and desktop.
